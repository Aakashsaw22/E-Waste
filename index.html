About E-waste..

E-waste refers to discarded electronic devices such as computers, mobile phones, televisions, printers, and other electronic equipment. Improper disposal of e-waste can harm the environment and human health due to the presence of harmful chemicals and materials. Proper management and recycling of e-waste help reduce pollution and conserve natural resources.



Why E-waste Management is Important

E-waste management is important because electronic waste can release harmful chemicals into the soil, water, and air. Proper recycling helps recover valuable materials and reduces environmental damage. Managing e-waste responsibly also saves natural resources and energy.
